# ContinuEd 📚

### Your Learning, Always in Continuity.

> **Life happens. Learning continues.**

ContinuEd is an AI-powered academic continuity platform designed to help students catch up when they miss a class.

Missing a class should not mean missing the learning. ContinuEd brings together everything a student missed, including topics, notes, assignments, resources, and announcements, and uses AI to help them understand what to study and how to catch up.

---

## 🌱 Why ContinuEd?

Students miss classes for many reasons. Emergencies, health issues, college events, personal responsibilities, or simply being unavailable for a day.

The usual process is often:

> "What did I miss?"

Then students have to search through WhatsApp messages, ask classmates for notes, find assignments, and figure out what topics they need to study first.

ContinuEd brings all of this into one place.

Instead of only showing students what they missed, ContinuEd helps them understand it and get back on track.

---

## 💡 What ContinuEd Does

ContinuEd connects teachers and students through a simple learning continuity workflow.

### 👩‍🏫 For Teachers

Teachers can share:

- Topics covered in class
- Important points
- Class notes
- PPTs and study material
- Assignments
- Reference links
- Announcements

### 👨‍🎓 For Students

Students can:

- View their classes
- See classes they missed
- Check what was taught
- Access notes and resources
- View pending assignments
- Get AI-generated summaries
- Generate practice questions
- Follow personalized catch-up plans
